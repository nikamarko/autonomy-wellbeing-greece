About the method

Self-sufficiency is the share of electricity demand met on site in the same hour it is needed, after the battery. Unlike an annual net-zero balance, it shows how much the building still depends on the grid at night and in winter. Self-consumption is the share of solar output used in the building rather than exported, which matters under Greece's net-billing scheme.

Well-being combines passive thermal comfort (EN 16798-1 adaptive range, without mechanical cooling or heating), daylight, freedom from glare, morning melanopic light (CIE S 026), view out (EN 17037), fresh-air hours and your team's qualitative scores. The weights are yours to set.

Why it matters now: under the 2024 EU buildings directive (EPBD recast), new buildings must be zero-emission from 2030 (public buildings from 2028), solar installations become mandatory on new buildings, and indoor environmental quality becomes a legal objective. The question shifts from whether to install PV to how to use it well while keeping occupants healthy.

About this model. This is a reduced-order demonstrator of the research method, not a certified calculation. Weather for each of the 69 locations is synthesised from approximate monthly climate normals with day-to-day variability; heating degree days match published values for the zone reference cities within −8% to +3%. KENAK zones follow the official prefecture table (TOTEE 20701-3, 2017 revision). Heat flows use a single-zone 1R1C model with ideal heat pumps; passive comfort uses the same model free-running with window and night ventilation against the EN 16798-1 adaptive range. Daylight, glare, morning (melanopic) light and view are simplified proxies of EN 17037 and CIE S 026 metrics. U-values for "KENAK min." approximate the 2017 zone limits. The 2050 case is a uniform +1.8 °C (winter) to +2.5 °C (summer) shift. The seasonal hotel operates April–October with a winter frost setback. Battery dispatch is rule-based (use, then store, then export). Verify any design decision with EnergyPlus/Radiance simulation.
