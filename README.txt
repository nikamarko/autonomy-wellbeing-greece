Energy Autonomy and Human Well-Being in Greek Buildings
Interactive design tool - Marko Nika, 2026

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip this folder anywhere on your computer.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser
   (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). No installation is needed.
3. The tool runs entirely on your computer. An internet connection
   is only used to load the display fonts; without it, standard
   fonts are used and everything still works.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- index.html   the interactive tool (69 locations across Greece)
- paper.pdf    the research paper describing the method

USING THE TOOL
- Choose a location from the list or click it on the map.
- Change the building's form, envelope, shading, solar PV and battery.
- Results update instantly: hourly self-sufficiency, energy balance,
  and six well-being indicators.
- "Generate 300 variants" explores the design space and finds the
  best trade-offs between energy autonomy and well-being.
- "Save current design" lets you compare scenarios side by side.
- "Download results (CSV)" exports the current scenario for Excel.
- "Print or save report as PDF" produces a printable summary.

PUTTING IT ONLINE
Upload index.html and paper.pdf to any static web host (for example
GitHub Pages, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages) to share it with a link.

IMPORTANT
Results come from a simplified hourly model with approximate climate
data. They are indicative and intended for early-design exploration
and education. They do not replace the official KENAK calculation or
a detailed EnergyPlus/Radiance study.
